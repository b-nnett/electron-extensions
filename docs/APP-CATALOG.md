# App candidate catalog

Research and artifact snapshot: 5 September 2026. This lists the **40 additional candidates** in [the app catalog](../compatibility/apps.json), beyond the original nine. **All 40 now have shipped Electron-framework evidence:** 39 from installed bundles or signature/fingerprint-matched installation copies, and Rancher Desktop from its verified read-only vendor image. The original source research established 37; the installation reports below resolve Miro, Evernote and Notion Calendar. See [the installation reports](../output/installations/), [Rancher's image preparation metadata](../output/compatibility/rancher/image-test-preparation.json), and [the current results](../compatibility/results.json) for the associated artifacts and versions. Rancher was not permanently installed.

This is a candidate inventory, not a popularity ranking or a runtime compatibility report. “Confirmed” refers to Electron evidence only. A source manifest establishes the implementation in that source revision; its Electron version may differ from the downloaded binary. Installed-framework evidence applies to the inspected app version.

Popularity measures are not comparable: GitHub stars measure project interest, Homebrew counts cover only that installation channel, and vendor figures may span products, platforms or many years. None establishes current macOS active users. Approximate figures are marked ≈; dates and scope are retained where material.
